# DevRoadmap: Thought Leadership for Career Growth

DevRoadmap is not a "learning journal" in the traditional sense. It's a **thought leadership platform** designed to showcase deep technical thinking, cross-disciplinary problem-solving. It is a curated collection of essays on **go-to-market strategy, technical product positioning, and building products that matter**, written by someone who has shipped products at P&G, Fintech, Blockchain, and high-growth SaaS.

## 🎯 What This Is

This is not a blog. It's a **working notebook** for Product Marketing professionals, technical decision-makers, and founders who care about **how products actually reach customers**.

Each essay tackles a real problem in product marketing:
- How to position technical products for non-technical buyers
- Why most go-to-market strategies fail (and what actually works)
- How to build product value propositions that resonate across buyer personas
- The psychology behind what makes technical products successful
- How to bridge the gap between engineering and business

## 🤔 Who This Is For

**Primary Audience:**
- **Product Marketing Managers** building GTM strategies for technical products
- **Startup founders** who need to communicate product value clearly
- **Technical leaders** who understand that positioning is as critical as execution

## 💡 Why This Matters

Most product marketing resources are either:
1. **Too academic** — divorced from real shipping decisions
2. **Too tactical** — focused on channel optimization, not strategy
3. **Too generic** — written for B2C e-commerce, not technical products

This is different. Every essay is written from **real experience launching products at scale**:
- 23% YoY revenue growth on a $15M+ FMCG portfolio
- 40% YoY growth on a blockchain SaaS platform
- €250K+ expansion ARR campaigns at high-growth SaaS
- GTM strategies across East Africa, APAC, EMEA, and LATAM

**The insight:** Go-to-market isn't about tactics. It's about understanding your customer's actual problem and translating it into language they understand.

## 🏗️ The Philosophy

### 1. **Great Marketing Comes From Understanding, Not Tricks**
There are no "growth hacks" that overcome a weak value proposition. But there are clear frameworks that clarify what you're actually selling.

### 2. **Technical Products Deserve Technical Thinking**
You can't market Blockchain like you market shampoo. Distribution, buyer dynamics, and messaging opportunities are fundamentally different. This explores those nuances.

### 3. **The Best Insights Come From Shipping**
Every essay is grounded in real experience:
- Negotiating with tier-1 retail partners (Carrefour, Naivas)
- Launching B2B SaaS in emerging markets
- Building trust in nascent tech ecosystems (Blockchain, DeFi)
- Managing $10M+ marketing budgets with clear ROI accountability

That real-world context informs every insight here.
